# Shell — Bay-window sitting room

A modest sitting room in an older house. Calm, well proportioned, with one feature worth coveting: a deep bay window with a built-in seat.

## The room

- **Size:** about 4.4 m wide and 5.2 m deep. Ceiling 2.9 m.
- **Detail:** a simple plaster cornice, a picture rail, tall plain skirting boards. Plain painted plaster walls. No panelling, no arches, no niches.
- **Floor:** wide pale oak boards, matte.
- **Left wall:** the bay window. Three tall sash windows in a shallow three-sided bay, with a built-in window seat about 45 cm high running its full width. This is the only source of light.
- **Far wall:** plain. A panelled door at its right-hand end, standing slightly open onto a dim hall.
- **Right wall:** plain and unbroken. The sofa stands against it.
- **No fireplace.**

## Furniture positions

- A three-seat sofa against the right wall, facing the bay.
- One armchair near the far left corner, angled toward the sofa.
- A low coffee table between them, about 45 cm from the sofa.
- A small side table at the far end of the sofa with one table lamp, switched off.
- A flat-woven rug under the seating group, front legs of the sofa and chair on it.
- A clear path about 90 cm wide from the door, along the far wall, to both seats and to the window seat.

## Camera

Standing just inside the near end of the room, a little right of centre, looking down its length. Lens height about 1.3 m. An ordinary moderate wide lens, roughly 28–35 mm. The bay window is on the left of the picture, the sofa on the right, the open door at the far right. Some ceiling and a generous stretch of floor are in frame. Vertical lines are vertical.

## Light

Soft overcast daylight through the bay, late morning. It falls across the room from left to right; the right wall is gently darker than the left. No artificial light.

Oak floor, off-white lampshade, off-white ceramics, books with pale spines. The lampshade is the white reference: it must look white.
