# Project 001 — Wada #231, sitting room

Read [the Guide](/guide) first. This page is the direction for one image.

## Palette — Wada #231

| Colour | Value | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| Cameo Pink | `#E0B3B6` | A pale, cool, dusty pink. Not peach, not salmon, not beige |
| Hay's Russet | `#793327` | A deep red-brown, like oxblood leather or old brick. Not orange, not chocolate |
| Olympic Blue | `#5A82B3` | A clear medium blue, like a cornflower. Not navy, not grey, not teal |

The idea: a soft pink room, anchored by one deep russet piece, woken up by a clear blue.

## Room shell

[Bay-window sitting room](/shells/bay-window-sitting-room). Follow its room, furniture positions, camera and light exactly.

## Colour placement

| Colour | Where | Share of the picture |
|---|---|---|
| **Cameo Pink** | Walls, ceiling, cornice, picture rail, skirting, window frames and the window-seat joinery, all painted the same matte pink | about 55–60% |
| **Hay's Russet** | The sofa, in matte wool or linen-velvet. Nothing else large | about 15–20% |
| **Olympic Blue** | The window-seat cushion, the ceramic lamp base, and one folded wool throw over the sofa arm | about 8–10% |
| Supporting neutrals | Pale oak floor; off-white flat-woven rug; armchair in natural undyed linen; off-white lampshade; pale stone or oak coffee table | the rest |

The pink must stay pink in the lit areas and in the shade. The blue must stay clearly blue, not slate.

## Traces of life

- A book left open, face down, on the blue window-seat cushion.
- The blue throw folded loosely, not squarely.
- One sofa cushion slightly dented.
- A cup and a pair of reading glasses on the coffee table.
- The hall door ajar.

Nothing else on the surfaces except two or three books and one small branch in a plain off-white jug.

## Must not

- No lamps or other artificial light switched on. No warm colour cast.
- No fireplace, panelling, arches, niches, chandelier or ceiling rose.
- No second sofa, no matching pair of chairs.
- No patterned fabrics or patterned rug.
- No artwork on the walls.
- No colours beyond the three palette colours and the listed neutrals. No brass or gold.
- No text, people or animals.

## The prompt

Use as written.

> A vertical 9:16 interior photograph of a modest sitting room in an older house, taken by a photographer standing just inside the room, a little right of centre, camera at chest height, 30 mm lens, vertical lines perfectly straight.
>
> The room is about 4.4 metres wide and 5.2 metres deep with a 2.9 metre ceiling, a simple plaster cornice, a picture rail and tall plain skirting boards. On the left wall is a deep bay of three tall sash windows with a built-in window seat. The far wall is plain, with a panelled door at its right end standing slightly open onto a dim hall. The right wall is plain. The floor is wide, pale, matte oak boards. There is no fireplace and no wall panelling.
>
> The walls, ceiling, cornice, picture rail, skirting, window frames and window seat are all painted the same matte pale dusty cool pink, Cameo Pink (#E0B3B6): clearly pink, not peach or beige.
>
> Against the right wall, facing the windows, stands a three-seat sofa upholstered in matte deep red-brown wool, Hay's Russet (#793327), the colour of oxblood leather. One armchair in natural undyed linen sits near the far left corner, angled toward the sofa. Between them is a low, pale coffee table on a plain off-white flat-woven rug.
>
> The window-seat cushion is a clear medium cornflower blue wool, Olympic Blue (#5A82B3). At the far end of the sofa a small side table holds a lamp with a ceramic base in the same blue and a plain white shade; the lamp is switched off. A wool throw in the same blue is folded loosely over the near arm of the sofa.
>
> A book lies open, face down, on the window seat. One sofa cushion is slightly dented. A cup and a pair of reading glasses sit on the coffee table beside two books and a single branch in a plain off-white jug. Nothing hangs on the walls.
>
> Soft, neutral, overcast late-morning daylight comes only from the bay windows on the left and falls across the room; the right wall is gently darker. No artificial light, no warm or golden cast: the white lampshade looks pure white. Natural, realistic, slightly imperfect, like an editorial photograph for an interiors magazine. No people, no text, no patterns, no brass or gold, no colours other than the pink, the russet, the blue, pale oak and off-white.
